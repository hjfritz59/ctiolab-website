What's included in every plan

No feature tiers and no add-ons.

  • Sales-based reorder forecasts
  • Purchase orders, PDF and email to suppliers
  • Barcode receiving and stock counts
  • Supplier import from spreadsheets
  • Multiple locations and drop-ship orders
  • Dashboard, best sellers and low-stock alerts
What counts as an active SKU?

Each product variant from your Shopify catalog that the app actively tracks counts as one SKU.

What if I grow past my plan's limit?

Nothing stops working. The app lets you know and asks you to move to the next plan.

How does billing work?

Shopify handles billing. You approve the plan in your Shopify admin and it appears on your regular Shopify invoice.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, at any time from your Shopify admin.
